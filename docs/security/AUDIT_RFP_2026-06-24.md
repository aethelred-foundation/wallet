## 3. Why now / context

The wallet is feature-complete at the design layer; the audit is the gating
item for production and enterprise procurement (and a prerequisite evidence
artifact for SOC-2 Type 1). Recent additions since the scope doc that the
auditor should include:

- **ERC-4337 v0.6 + v0.7** smart-account paths (recent ToB research flagged six
  recurring 4337 vulnerability patterns — please confirm coverage).
- **EIP-7702** authorization primitive (`packages/smart-account/src/eip7702.ts`).
- **MPC-TSS adapter** (`packages/custody-adapters/src/mpc-tss-adapter.ts`) — the
  recovery cross-check is the load-bearing control.
- **Live screening circuit breaker** and **social recovery** (`SocialRecoveryModule`).

## 4. Highest-priority review targets

1. Hand-rolled **EIP-712 v4** encoders (`custody-adapters/eip712-hash.ts`).
2. **Shamir 2-of-2** split/reconstruct over the secp256k1 order.
3. **MPC-TSS** signature handling + the recovery cross-check.
4. **EIP-7702** authorization hashing + recovery (replay / substitution).
5. **TEE attestation binding** — `bindingHash = sha256(structHash ‖ sha256(canonicalQuoteBytes))`.
6. **AgentBudget.sol** atomic revocation + **Notary.sol** Merkle anchoring.
7. ERC-4337 v0.6/v0.7 UserOperation handling.

## 5. What your proposal should include

1. Availability for a **Q3 2026** slot (slots at top firms fill 6–8 weeks out).
2. **Fixed-fee** quote with calendar timeline and a deliverable list.
3. Team composition (principal + associate) and relevant track record on
   **ECDSA / MPC-TSS / TEE-attestation-binding** reviews.
4. Your approach to reviewing **hand-rolled ABI/EIP-712 encoders** vs library-backed.
5. Coverage depth on the **Shamir / MPC** paths — malformed-share / malicious-cohort
   cases, not just happy-path.
6. Whether you offer a **retainer** for incremental re-review post-engagement.
7. Confirmation you'll deliver a **public-shareable redacted** final report.
