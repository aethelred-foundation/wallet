## 1. Purpose

This document defines how enterprise mode moves from internal readiness into design-partner validation and enterprise beta.

It exists to prevent two common Phase 2 mistakes:

- onboarding partners before enterprise controls are stable enough to support them
- calling something “enterprise beta” without clear partner scope, evidence standards, and release gates

## 2. Enterprise Design-Partner Objective

Enterprise design partners should validate:

- whether workspaces, roles, approvals, and policy actually solve real operating pain
- whether audit and evidence outputs are useful outside engineering
- whether the wallet can support meaningful enterprise workflows without internal heroics

## 3. Design-Partner Entry Criteria

Partner onboarding should begin only when:

- workspace and approval workflows are usable
- evidence export v1 exists
- support ownership is named
- known control gaps are documented
- partner success metrics are agreed in advance

## 4. Recommended Partner Profile

Best early enterprise partners:

- teams with real treasury or workflow approval needs
- partners willing to work in a controlled beta
- teams close enough to Aethelred that support loops can be tight

Avoid first:

- highly bespoke partners needing sovereign deployment guarantees
- partners with no measurable workflow goal
- partners expecting every enterprise feature before controlled validation

## 5. Design Partner 1 Objective

Partner 1 should validate:

- first meaningful treasury or approval workflow
- clarity of approval and review surfaces
- usefulness of evidence exports

## 6. Design Partner 2 Objective

Partner 2 should validate:

- a meaningfully different enterprise workflow
- broader fit beyond the first partner’s exact use case
- repeatability of onboarding and support

## 7. Partner Onboarding Pack

The partner pack should include:

- supported workflows
- known limitations
- onboarding instructions
- support channel
- escalation path
- review cadence
- expected feedback format

## 8. Enterprise Beta Objective

Enterprise beta should prove:

- enterprise mode is stable enough for controlled repeated use
- high-risk approval and admin paths are understood
- the support team can handle partner issues quickly
- the product is ready to form the base for sovereign-grade work later

## 9. Beta Entry Criteria

Enterprise beta begins only when:

- threat model is updated
- approval abuse cases are retested
- admin misuse cases are reviewed
- beta release gate is explicit
- the team can rehearse partner support and evidence review end to end

## 10. Partner Success Metrics

Measure at minimum:

- workflow completion rate
- approval completion rate
- policy coverage on sensitive actions
- evidence export usability
- support burden per partner
- repeat-use reliability

## 11. Beta Review Inputs

The enterprise beta review should include:

- partner 1 results
- partner 2 results
- defect severity trends
- approval and policy reliability
- evidence export quality
- admin and compliance usability
- security review findings

## 12. Phase 2 Exit Criteria

Phase 2 should exit only when:

- at least two design partners have completed meaningful activity
- enterprise controls are credible in real workflows
- beta issues are controlled enough for Phase 3 progression
- the program can explain exactly what is ready and what still belongs to later phases
